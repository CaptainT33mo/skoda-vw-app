## Full description (max 4000)

```
CarPulse shows your connected car's live data — without ads, tracking or clutter. It works with Skoda and Volkswagen cars in India that are enrolled in the carmakers' connected-car service with the plug-in telematics device, and it is open source.

LIVE DATA
• Instrument cluster with speed, RPM, fuel, battery voltage, coolant and intake temperature, engine load and odometer
• Real-time updates while you drive, within seconds of the car reporting
• Check-engine light status and distance since the last diagnostics reset

TRIPS & DRIVING
• Trip history with start and end places, distance, duration and driving score
• Weekly stats, top speed, idle time, hard braking and your real-world mileage (km/l)
• Find trips by date

ANDROID AUTO
• A live dashboard on your car's screen, designed to sit next to Maps in split screen
• Optional speed, fuel and odometer straight from your car's head unit

CAR ALERTS (OPTIONAL)
• Battery running low while parked
• Check-engine light comes on
• Engine started in the middle of the night
• Fuel low

FIND & LOCATE
• Where your car is parked, on a map, with one tap to Google Maps
• Search for service centres, showrooms, petrol pumps or parking near your car
• Save your own service centre with one-tap directions and calls

MORE
• Several cars on one account, with a quick switcher
• Home-screen widgets that follow your phone's light/dark theme
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Privacy mode that hides your VIN, registration and personal details for screenshots
• Try it first with the built-in demo car — no account needed

PRIVACY
CarPulse has no server of its own: your data goes only between your phone and the carmakers' own connected-car servers, just as with the official apps. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The source code is public.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Skoda or Volkswagen car in India with an active connected-car subscription and the telematics device fitted
• The mobile number registered on that account (sign-in is by SMS one-time password)
• Volkswagen support is new: please report any sign-in problems on GitHub

CarPulse is an independent project. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Škoda Auto, Volkswagen AG or Škoda Auto Volkswagen India. "Skoda", "Škoda", "Volkswagen" and model names are trademarks of their owners and are used only to say which cars the app works with.
```
